About the project

Client-facing web application for real estate investment subscriptions. I have contributed to it since its early development stage.

How it works

As a QA engineer, I worked on a client-facing application for real estate investment. Clients complete a long, multi-step subscription.

The application is in French, so the steps keep their French names with a short English description.

The subscription path

  1. ObjectifsChoose the profile (Individual, Legal Entity or Joint Subscription), then set investment goals and amount
  2. InformationsPersonal information and tax addresses
  3. SituationFinancial situation
  4. AdéquationSuitability assessment
  5. ConseilsEstimated investment returns and projections
  6. DocumentsUpload the required files, such as proof of identity and proof of address
  7. SignatureSign the documents to finalize the subscription
  8. PaiementChoose the payment method

After the signature, the internal team approves or rejects the subscription. Once approved, clients can invest again and track their investments and projected income on their dashboard.

When I joined

I joined at the early stage of development, when there were no specifications, and I wrote them.
