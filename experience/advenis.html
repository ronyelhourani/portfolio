About the project

Web application for real estate investment subscriptions, used by financial advisors to complete subscriptions on behalf of their clients.

How it works

To make a real estate investment for a client, the financial advisor must first create the client's account and start the subscription process on their behalf as an Individual, Legal Entity, or Joint Subscription.

Throughout the subscription process, the advisor completes several steps with the client's information, including personal information, investment objectives, financial situation, a suitability assessment, bank account details to receive investment returns, and the required supporting documents (proof of identity, proof of address, etc.).

Once all the steps are completed, the required documents are reviewed and electronically signed before the subscription is submitted.

The internal team then reviews and verifies the submitted information and documents to determine whether the subscription should be approved or rejected.

During the subscription process, the advisor can view the client's estimated investment returns and financial projections to help guide their decision.

Once the subscription is approved, the advisor can make additional investments for the client and access the dashboard to track their investments and view their projected income over the coming months and years.

About the project

Crowdfunding web application that connects investors with businesses and projects seeking funding.

How it works

Project owners submit their investment projects to WiSEED, providing all the necessary details and supporting documents. The WiSEED team reviews and evaluates each project before deciding whether to approve or reject it. Once approved, the project is published on the WiSEED platform, allowing investors to explore it and invest their desired amount.

To invest in a project, clients must first complete several steps:

  • Account Creation: Create a personal account on the WiSEED platform.
  • KYC Verification: Complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) process by providing personal information, financial details, a suitability assessment, and the required supporting documents.
  • Wallet Activation: Once the KYC is approved, clients gain access to a digital wallet, which allows them to deposit funds, manage their balance, receive investment returns, and withdraw money.

After their account and wallet have been validated, clients can invest in the available projects on WiSEED and potentially earn returns based on each project's conditions and performance.

Throughout the investment process, clients can view estimated investment returns and financial projections for the coming months and years, helping them evaluate potential opportunities and make informed investment decisions.

However, investing in these projects involves financial risks. A project may underperform or fail, potentially resulting in a partial or total loss of the investor's invested capital. Therefore, investment returns are not guaranteed, and investors should carefully evaluate the risks before making an investment decision.
