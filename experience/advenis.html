About the product

Web application for real estate investment subscriptions, used by financial advisors to complete subscriptions on behalf of their clients.

How it works

As a QA engineer, I worked on a web application used by financial advisors to subscribe real estate investments on behalf of their clients.

The financial advisor

  • Creates the client as an Individual, Legal Entity or Joint Subscription
  • Completes a long multi-step subscription. The path differs from AWM, but the information is the same:
    Personal information Investment objectives Financial situation Suitability assessment Bank account details Files to provide
  • Signs the documents

The internal team

  • Reviews the information and documents
  • Approves or rejects the subscription

The application was already developed when I joined, with many bugs and incomplete specifications. I fixed the specs and kept them up to date.
