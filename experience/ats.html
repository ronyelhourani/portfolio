Project

Middleware XL EDS for Chronopost

Chronopost is a well-known parcel delivery company in France. The middleware is an integration layer: clients send requests and order pricing through a web interface or an API, and it forwards them to Urbantz, a delivery management platform used through a mobile application.

How it works

Clients can create delivery orders through two different channels:

  • API Integration: Primarily used by large companies that process thousands of orders every week. This method enables faster, automated order creation and seamless integration with their existing systems.
  • Web Portal: Designed for clients who manage a smaller volume of orders and prefer to create and manage them manually through a user-friendly interface.

During order creation, clients provide all the necessary information, including the type of goods, weight, pickup and delivery addresses, preferred delivery date and time, and any additional delivery requirements.

Once an order is created, it is registered in Middleware XLEDS, which centralizes and manages order information, and transmitted to Urbantz, a last-mile delivery management platform responsible for organizing and tracking deliveries.

Through Urbantz, delivery orders are assigned to drivers, who use a mobile application to access their assigned deliveries, view destination addresses, and navigate using maps. Drivers can also update the delivery status in real time, indicating whether an order has been successfully delivered or whether the delivery has failed, along with the reason for the failure.

These status updates are then synchronized with Middleware XLEDS, ensuring that delivery information remains accurate and up to date across the different systems.

This integration enables efficient order processing, centralized tracking, and seamless communication between clients, the middleware, the delivery management platform, and drivers.
